About Us

This website was made to help people know more about Filipino culture and our heritage. It talks about different traditions, values, beliefs, foods, and events from the past that helped shape who Filipinos are today. We want to show the importance of our culture and encourage others, especially young people, to learn about and appreciate it.

RESPECT AND FAMILY

Filipinos love their families very much and show great respect to older people every day. Families are very close, and grandparents, parents, and children often live together in one big home to help each other. To show respect, young people do a gesture called pagmamano, where they gently take an elder's hand and touch it to their own forehead to get a blessing. They also use special, polite words like po and opo when talking to adults, and always make sure to take care of their relatives as they grow old..

SUPERSTITION

Filipino superstitions are traditional beliefs passed down from one generation to another. They often involve luck, protection, and everyday activities.

Historical Context

The history of the Philippines is shaped by indigenous traditions and influences from different cultures. These influences helped create the unique Filipino identity we know today.

Colonizers greatly influenced the development of Filipino culture by introducing foreign religions, languages, traditions, food, and systems of government. Instead of completely replacing indigenous practices, these influences blended with existing Filipino customs and created a unique cultural fusion. Spanish colonization influenced Christianity, festivals, names, architecture, and language, while American colonization introduced English, public education, and new forms of entertainment. This cultural fusion can still be seen in Filipino food, music, celebrations, and daily expressions. However, colonization also caused the loss and suppression of some indigenous traditions. Therefore, Filipino culture reflects both the resilience of native communities and the lasting influence of foreign colonizers.

Our Team

Welcome to our community! We are a passionate group of students dedicated to celebrating and sharing the rich beauty, vibrant traditions, and deep history of Filipino culture. As shown in our journey, we bring this mission to life through collaboration and creative teamwork, whether we are sketching out ideas, mapping out cultural projects on sticky notes, or engaging in lively group discussions.
